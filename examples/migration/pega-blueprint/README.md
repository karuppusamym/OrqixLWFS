# Blueprint PDF fixtures

Two blueprint PDFs are used by the tests and demos. See [S91](../../../docs/build/S91-pega-blueprint-pdf.md).

## Genuine Pega Blueprint export

`RetailLoanOrigination.pdf` is a genuine Pega Blueprint **"Application Overview Document"** export (Blueprint ID
BP-63442, (c) 2024 Pegasystems), added at the owner's request. Its own notice says the contents are confidential and
provided for use with Pegasystems applications; keep that in mind before sharing this repository outside the team.

It defines three case types (Home Loan Application, Unsecured Retail Loan Application, Secured Retail Loan
Application), six personas and seven Live Data objects. The Pega Blueprint export layout (`pega-blueprint 0.1`) reads
it from page geometry: stage columns, step cards and their icon colours, field tables, personas and Live Data.

`RetailLoanOrigination.transcription.json` is the expected transcription of the first case type (Home Loan
Application): 8 stages, 58 steps, 6 decisions, 24 fields, 6 roles and 7 integrations, every element `proposed`. It is
asserted by `PegaBlueprintLayoutTest`; regenerate it after a deliberate layout change with `-Dblueprint.regenerate=true`
on that test.

What a reviewer adds, because the export does not state it: a role for each kept step, routes and rejection paths,
conditions for the decisions, outcomes (for example **Approved** and **Rejected**), and SLAs. Automated steps, decisions
and integrations are excluded with a reason and rebuilt in the studio. To migrate another case type, upload the PDF
again and enter its name in **Case type**.

## Synthetic outline sample (NOT a Pega export)

`synthetic-blueprint.pdf` is a **synthetic** three-page application-blueprint outline written for Orqix tests and demos.
It is generated by [`tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py`](../../../tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py)
(pure Python standard library, byte-for-byte deterministic). It does **not** reproduce the layout of Pega's own Blueprint
PDF export and is read by the generic outline layout.

`synthetic-blueprint.transcription.json` is the expected transcription the generic outline layout
(`generic-outline` 0.1) produces from it: every element `proposed`, with its source page and source line. It is asserted by
`BlueprintPipelineTest` and `PegaBlueprintApiTest`. Regenerate it after a deliberate layout change with
`-Dblueprint.regenerate=true` on `BlueprintPipelineTest`.

The sample deliberately leaves gaps a reviewer must resolve:

- the Finance approval SLA states `8` without a unit (finding `BLUEPRINT_SLA_UNIT_MISSING`);
- the purchase-order integration has no target and integrations are not converted (`BLUEPRINT_INTEGRATION_UNSUPPORTED`,
  `BLUEPRINT_INTEGRATION_TARGET_MISSING`); exclude it with a reason;
- title and notes lines are not blueprint elements (`BLUEPRINT_TEXT_UNCLASSIFIED`), stages are informational.

After review: map **Line Manager** to `managers` and **Finance Officer** to `finance-approvers`. Amounts of 5,000 or more go
to Finance approval after the manager; lower amounts finish as **Approved** after the manager; any rejection finishes as
**Rejected**. Due times are 48 hours (manager) and whatever unit the reviewer records for finance (the tests use 8 hours).

Regenerate the PDF: `python tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py`.
