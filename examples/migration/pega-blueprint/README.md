# Synthetic blueprint PDF (NOT a Pega export)

`synthetic-blueprint.pdf` is a **synthetic** three-page application-blueprint outline written for Orqix tests and demos. It is
generated by [`tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py`](../../../tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py)
(pure Python standard library, byte-for-byte deterministic). It does **not** reproduce the layout of Pega's own Blueprint PDF
export, and nothing here claims compatibility with that export. No genuine Pega Blueprint sample exists in this repository.

`synthetic-blueprint.transcription.json` is the expected transcription the generic outline layout
(`generic-outline 0.1`) produces from it: every element `proposed`, with its source page and source line. It is asserted by
`BlueprintPipelineTest` and `PegaBlueprintApiTest`. Regenerate it after a deliberate layout change with
`-Dblueprint.regenerate=true` on `BlueprintPipelineTest`.

The sample deliberately leaves gaps a reviewer must resolve:

- the Finance approval SLA states `8` without a unit (finding `BLUEPRINT_SLA_UNIT_MISSING`);
- the purchase-order integration has no target and integrations are not converted (`BLUEPRINT_INTEGRATION_UNSUPPORTED`,
  `BLUEPRINT_INTEGRATION_TARGET_MISSING`); exclude it with a reason;
- title and notes lines are not blueprint elements (`BLUEPRINT_TEXT_UNCLASSIFIED`), stages are informational.

After review: map **Line Manager** to `managers` and **Finance Officer** to `finance-approvers`. Amounts of 5,000 or more go
to Finance approval after the manager; lower amounts finish as **Approved** after the manager; any rejection finishes as
**Rejected**. Due times are 48 hours (manager) and whatever unit the reviewer records for finance (the tests use 8 hours).

Regenerate the PDF: `python tools/blueprint-sample/make_synthetic_blueprint.py`. See
[S91](../../../docs/build/S91-pega-blueprint-pdf.md).
